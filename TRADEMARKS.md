The logos and brand assets in this repository incorporate trademarks owned by
**Google LLC** — including the **Google Developer Groups (GDG)**, **Women Techmakers**,
**Build with AI**, and **DevFest** marks — combined with community identifiers for Sri Lanka.

## You may

- Use these assets to promote and represent the respective Sri Lanka communities and their
  official events, meetups, and activities.
- Use them in presentations, slides, social media posts, websites, blog posts, and event
  collateral that relate to these communities.

## You may not

- Alter, distort, recolor, rotate, or add effects to the logos.
- Use the marks in a way that implies Google's or the community's endorsement of an
  unrelated product, service, company, or event.
- Use the marks for commercial merchandise without permission from the community organizers.
- Register these marks (or confusingly similar ones) as your own.

## Google brand guidelines

Because these assets build on Google's community program marks, their use is also subject
to Google's brand and trademark policies:

- Google Developer Groups: https://developers.google.com/community/gdg
- Women Techmakers: https://developers.google.com/womentechmakers

## Questions

If you're unsure whether your intended use is allowed, please reach out to the
GDG Sri Lanka organizers before publishing.
